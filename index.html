01 / PROFILE

Research at the intersection of devices, circuits & intelligent hardware.

Dr. Deepika Gupta is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at IIIT Naya Raipur. Her academic background spans Electrical Engineering, VLSI Design, and Electronics & Communication Engineering.

Her doctoral work at IIT Indore focused on charge-trap NAND flash memory and improved reliability. Her current research interests include hardware security system design based on advanced non-volatile memories, memristor emulator design, 3D charge-trap NAND flash memory, advanced MOS devices, and digital circuit and logic design.
